Site & Contact

Your information

We respect your privacy. If you submit the contact form, we store only the information necessary to respond. We do not sell your data. For analytics, we may use a privacy-friendly tool (e.g., Plausible) without cookies.

Partner Program

Referral partner applications

If you apply to our referral partner program, we review the information you submit together with publicly available professional information — for example, your LinkedIn profile — to assess fit. We use this information only to evaluate and respond to your application, share it only with the people at Texas Quantitative who review applications, and do not sell it.
