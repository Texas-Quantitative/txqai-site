# Formspree Setup Guide

## Step 1: Create Formspree Account
1. Go to https://formspree.io
2. Sign up for a free account (allows 50 submissions per month)
3. Verify your email address

## Step 2: Create Your Form
1. In your Formspree dashboard, click "New Form"
2. Name it something like "Texas Quantitative Contact Form"
3. Copy the form endpoint URL (it will look like: `https://formspree.io/f/xvgpkmlq`)

## Step 3: Update Your Website
1. Open `contact.html` 
2. Find this line: `action="https://formspree.io/f/YOUR_FORM_ID"`
3. Replace `YOUR_FORM_ID` with your actual Formspree form ID
4. Example: `action="https://formspree.io/f/xvgpkmlq"`

## Step 4: Test Your Form
1. Submit a test form on your website
2. Check your email for the submission
3. Check your Formspree dashboard for the submission

## What Happens When Someone Submits:
- ✅ You receive an email with their details
- ✅ Data is stored in your Formspree dashboard
- ✅ User sees a confirmation message
- ✅ Form includes spam protection
- ✅ Professional error handling
