02 / Raptor

Watch the agents do the work.

Raptor runs a software task through a crew of agents — a planner, workers, a reviewer, a QA gate. Most of it stays on local models. When a task is too hard, or too sensitive to send anywhere, the Escalation Boundary decides what happens next: it routes by difficulty and by classification, so a sensitive task never escalates off-site even when a harder one would.

Local tier

Runs on the box

Planning, implementation, review, and QA run on local models — on the client's own hardware. Nothing leaves.

Frontier tier

Escalates only when allowed

Hard reasoning can escalate to a frontier model — but only for tasks the classification policy permits.
